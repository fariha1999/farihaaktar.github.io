Site-Specific Adaptation Measures for Coastal Hazards in Cox's Bazar Sadar Upazila, Bangladesh

A group academic project assessing sea-level rise, salinity intrusion, shoreline erosion-accretion and storm-surge risk in Cox's Bazar Sadar Upazila, then translating the hazard assessment into locally relevant adaptation measures for coastal resilience.

Objective Evaluate key coastal hazards, project future sea-level-rise inundation for 2050 and 2100, and develop adaptation strategies grounded in local livelihoods, resources and regulatory frameworks.
Tools & data Google Earth Engine · Remote Sensing · NDSI · ArcGIS · BBS 2022 · Literature Review

Project context

Connecting coastal hazards with adaptation planning

Study area

Cox's Bazar Sadar Upazila is exposed to coastal flooding, storm surges, shoreline change and salinity intrusion. The project assessed these hazards together rather than treating them as isolated environmental problems.

Academic setting

Prepared as a five-member group project for Coastal Zone Management (WFM 6305) at BUET and submitted in September 2025.

Methodology

Multi-source coastal hazard assessment

01 Sea-level rise

Google Earth Engine was used to simulate future inundation scenarios for 2050 and 2100.

02 Salinity intrusion

NDSI was derived from remote-sensing data for 1994, 2004, 2014 and 2024.

03 Erosion-accretion

Remote-sensing outputs were analyzed and processed in ArcGIS to assess coastal change from 1994-2024.

04 Socio-institutional context

Storm-surge history, BBS data on resources and livelihoods, and coastal regulations were reviewed to shape adaptation measures.

Key findings

Hazard concentrations and future exposure

Salinity
Persistent intrusion zones

Bharuakhali, Pokhkhali and Jhilwanja were highlighted as important salinity-affected areas.

Future inundation
2050 → 2100 expansion

Projected inundation expands substantially by 2100, with Bharuakhali, Pokhkhali and Patail Machuakhali among the most exposed unions.

Coastal morphology
High recent change

The 2014-2024 period showed particularly strong erosion-accretion dynamics compared with earlier decades.

Adaptation strategy

Site-specific measures for coastal resilience

Hybrid coastal protection

Risk-sensitive setback policies, upgraded embankments and seawalls combined with mangroves and dune reinforcement.

Salinity management

Managed aquifer recharge, protective ponds, salt-tolerant crops, adjusted calendars and ecosystem restoration.

Cyclone & surge preparedness

Stronger early-warning systems, evacuation planning, resilient cyclone shelters and tidal-wetland buffers.

Livelihood & governance

Livelihood diversification, participatory governance, risk-sensitive land-use controls and scenario-based planning.

Project outcome

The project translated spatial hazard analysis into a coordinated adaptation framework combining structural protection, ecosystem restoration, livelihood resilience and institutional measures for Cox's Bazar Sadar Upazila.
