Neighborhood Plan: Annanya Residential Area

A neighborhood planning and design project for a 170-acre residential area in Chittagong, Bangladesh, integrating housing, education, commerce, health, community services, recreation, open space and a walkable street network into a complete residential neighborhood.

Objective Allocate required neighborhood facilities according to planning standards, explain their spatial placement, and develop an accessible and livable final plan for Annanya Residential Area.
Study area Annanya Residential Area · Chittagong · 170 acres
Planned population Approximately 60,000 residents
Design basis & tool Abashik Prokolper Bhumi Unnayan Bidhimala 2004 (amended 2015) · Neighborhood planning standards · AutoCAD

Project context

Designing a complete and accessible residential neighborhood

Planning challenge

The site is crossed by the Oxygen-Kuwaish Link Road and contains a north-south gas line. The plan had to accommodate a high residential population while distributing essential facilities so that daily needs remained accessible from each housing block.

Design direction

The neighborhood was structured around five blocks, a common node, a hierarchy of roads, bicycle facilities, mixed plot sizes, public facilities and substantial green/open space.

Methodology

From planning standards to spatial allocation

01 Review neighborhood principles

Neighborhood-unit concepts and international precedents were reviewed to identify core design principles.

02 Apply facility standards

Population-based standards were used to estimate the required area and quantity of education, health, commercial and community facilities.

03 Structure blocks & streets

Residential subdivisions, access roads, major roads, bicycle lanes and block patterns were organized in AutoCAD.

04 Allocate facilities

Facilities were distributed among Blocks A-E and the neighborhood node according to accessibility, safety and compatibility.

Neighborhood program

Facilities integrated into the plan

01Residential

3-, 5- and 7-katha plots with varied building heights and integrated housing types.

02Education

Nursery and primary schools, high schools, colleges and madrasas distributed across the blocks.

03Commerce

Local shops, super shops, kachabazar, shopping center, bank, ATMs and small service industries.

04Community & health

Clinics, pharmacy, clubs, community center, day care, hostels, orphanages, gymnasiums and old-age home.

05Open space & recreation

Parks, playing fields, courtyards, waterbodies, amusement space and green buffers.

Key design outcomes

A block-based plan organized around accessibility and daily life

Population basis
60,000 Residents planned for

The facility program and neighborhood design were prepared for an approximately 60,000-person population.

Residential structure
701 plots Three plot sizes

The plan includes 276 three-katha, 261 five-katha and 164 seven-katha residential plots.

Accessibility
5 blocks Distributed facilities

Education, retail, community and recreation facilities were distributed across Blocks A-E to reduce unnecessary travel.

Public realm
10 fields Open-space network

The plan also includes two waterbodies, parks, courtyards, playing fields, green buffers and peripheral greenery.

Study limitations

The project relied mainly on secondary sources and did not include a reconnaissance survey of the site or its surroundings. Some standards were unavailable in the Bangladesh context, leaving scope for future refinement using primary field data.

Project outcome

The final plan organizes housing, education, commerce, health, community services, recreation and open space into a coordinated neighborhood structure. The design emphasizes accessibility, walkability, safety, green space and a clear spatial identity for Annanya Residential Area.
