Transit-Oriented Development Potential Around Agargaon MRT Station

A comprehensive urban-planning project exploring the development potential of the one-kilometre influence area around Agargaon MRT Station, integrating existing land use, socio-economic conditions, mobility needs and Transit-Oriented Development principles into a proposed land-use and design framework.

Objective Explore the existing land-use pattern and socio-economic context around Agargaon MRT Station, then propose a land-use plan compatible with the station and the principles of compact, walkable and transit-supportive development.
Study area 1 km radius · Agargaon MRT Station · Ward 28, DNCC · 776 acres
Survey base 147 residential respondents · 94 commercial respondents
Methods & tools Reconnaissance · Questionnaire Survey · Literature Review · Microsoft Excel · GIS · R · Land-Use Design
Planning outputs
Land-Use Analysis & TOD Design Proposals

Project context

Planning for development around Dhaka's emerging MRT system

Existing condition

The study area combines residential, institutional, educational and restricted land uses, with extensive open land but limited publicly accessible parks. Existing sidewalks are weak, while the area has strong regional accessibility and major public institutions.

Planning direction

The project framed Agargaon as a potential Transit-Oriented Development area where land use, active transport, public space, housing and economic activity could be coordinated around MRT access.

Methodology

From existing conditions to a transit-oriented land-use proposal

01 Context & precedent review

Literature, international MRT/TOD cases and relevant Dhaka planning policies were reviewed to establish planning principles.

02 Field & questionnaire survey

Reconnaissance and separate residential/commercial questionnaires documented local conditions, needs, travel behavior and expectations.

03 Spatial & socio-economic analysis

Survey and land-use information were processed using Microsoft Excel, GIS and R to identify development potential and constraints.

04 TOD design proposal

Vision, objectives and strategic actions were translated into land-use, mobility, public-space, housing and economic design proposals.

Key findings

Development potential identified around Agargaon MRT

Existing land use
46% Open-space share

A large share of the area was classified as open space, but much of it was restricted rather than functioning as accessible neighborhood park space.

Redevelopment
78% Owners supported TOD redevelopment

Most surveyed residential landowners wanted Agargaon to develop as a TOD, although direct willingness to contribute land or resources was much lower.

Open-space demand
98.48% Residents wanted more open space

The project therefore treated public parks and accessible green space as a central component rather than leftover land.

Economic potential
94% Commercial respondents expected expansion

Commercial respondents showed strong interest in expanding activity after MRT operation, supporting mixed-use and commercial intensification near the station.

Study limitations

A substantial part of the study area was restricted and could not be surveyed; the sample was relatively small and not fully random; time and travel constrained data collection; and detailed up-to-date information for some parts of the study area was limited.

TOD strategy

Integrated land-use, mobility and public-realm proposals

01 · Compactness High-density & mixed land use

Promote block-based residential redevelopment and compatible mixed uses, especially within the 500 m transit influence area.

02 · Accessibility Walking & cycling network

Add bicycle lanes, bicycle parking, rickshaw facilities, improved sidewalks and safer pedestrian crossings.

03 · Inclusion Affordable housing

Retain access for low- and middle-income households through public housing and development incentives near transit.

04 · Environment Parks & green infrastructure

Transform underused open land into accessible parks, neighborhood green spaces and connected green strips.

05 · Economy Commercial hubs

Concentrate shops, offices, services, restaurants and public spaces close to the MRT station to create a self-sufficient center.

06 · Informality Designated vending space

Accommodate informal businesses at selected locations while preserving sufficient customer and pedestrian movement space.

07 · Public realm Vibrant station environment

Use public spaces, night-time activity and pedestrian-oriented design to support social interaction and safety near the station.

08 · Implementation Guided redevelopment

Use TOD principles, zoning and future redevelopment schemes to guide expected land-use change rather than allowing unplanned intensification.

Project outcome

The project produced an integrated TOD framework for Agargaon that connects land-use restructuring, housing, active transport, public space, environmental improvement and local economic activity. It demonstrates how MRT investment can be used as a catalyst for coordinated urban redevelopment rather than transport infrastructure alone.
