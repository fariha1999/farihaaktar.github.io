Formal Regionalization Based on Human Development Index: A District-Level Analysis in Bangladesh

A district-level regional planning project measuring human development across all 64 districts of Bangladesh and delineating formal regions to identify spatial disparities between developed, moderately developed and underdeveloped parts of the country.

Objective Calculate district-level HDI, compare human-development levels across Bangladesh, and explore the factors associated with developed and lagging regions.
Study area 64 districts of Bangladesh
HDI dimensions Health · Education · Standard of Living
Indicators & techniques Crude Death Rate · Literacy Rate · Household Consumption · Normalization · Geometric Mean · Formal Regionalization

Project context

Mapping human-development disparities at the district scale

Planning problem

National averages can conceal substantial regional differences. This project used a district-level approach to identify where human-development conditions are comparatively strong or weak across Bangladesh.

Regional perspective

The analysis linked HDI patterns with differences in urbanization, connectivity, economic opportunity, infrastructure, health and education access, poverty and exposure to natural hazards.

Methodology

Constructing HDI and delineating formal regions

01 Select HDI indicators

Crude death rate represented health, literacy rate represented education and household consumption represented standard of living.

02 Normalize indicators

Positive and negative indicators were transformed to comparable unitless indices, with a logarithmic transformation for consumption.

03 Calculate district HDI

The three normalized dimensions were combined using a geometric mean to generate an HDI value for each district.

04 Delineate formal regions

HDI values were tested with four classification techniques and grouped into spatial development categories for regional comparison.

Key findings

A clear spatial pattern of human-development disparity

Overall distribution
0.44 Mean district HDI

The calculated district-level index had a mean and median of 0.44, with substantial variation across Bangladesh.

Highest category
Dhaka · Gazipur · Feni Very high HDI

These districts formed the highest human-development category in the equal-interval classification.

Lowest category
Kurigram · Jamalpur · Joypurhat Very low HDI

The lowest category highlighted concentrated human-development disadvantages in northern and northwestern Bangladesh.

Regional pattern
East > West/Northwest Development is spatially uneven

Higher-HDI districts were concentrated more strongly in eastern and southeastern areas, while many western and northwestern districts lagged.

Study limitations

Indicator data came from different years because of data availability; urban-rural variation within individual districts was not captured; the index used only three parameters; and the analysis was cross-sectional rather than temporal.

Planning implications

Priority directions for reducing regional disparity

01 · Services Decentralize health & education

Expand tertiary health facilities and educational institutions beyond Dhaka and other leading districts.

02 · Regional investment Rebalance public resources

Improve funding and development allocation for lagging regions to support more balanced regional growth.

03 · Local economy Invest in local industries

Support locally appropriate industries and small and medium enterprises to create employment and reduce poverty.

04 · Connectivity Strengthen the northwest

Improve regional connectivity to expand trade and access to education, health and other services.

05 · Hazard resilience Target hazard-prone regions

Integrate adaptation and mitigation for coastal, flood, haor, char and drought-prone areas into regional development planning.

06 · Hill Tracts Improve basic infrastructure

Strengthen education, health and infrastructure in the Chittagong Hill Tracts while supporting sustainable local economic opportunities.

07 · Health investment Prioritize northern districts

Increase public health investment in districts with weak access to facilities and higher mortality-related disadvantages.

08 · Policy integration Embed human development in plans

Use human-development and social-protection objectives explicitly within national and regional planning frameworks.

Project outcome

The project produced a district-level HDI dataset and a formal regionalization of Bangladesh, showing where human-development advantages and disadvantages are spatially concentrated. It translated those patterns into regional planning priorities for infrastructure, social services, connectivity, economic opportunity and hazard-sensitive development.
