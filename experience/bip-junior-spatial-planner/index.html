Spatial Planning Framework

Contribution to the national SPF development process

Framework development

From expert inputs to a coherent planning document

My work supported the organization and refinement of the SPF as a practical framework for spatial planning in Bangladesh. I contributed to compilation and editing of draft material, coordination of specialist inputs, and preparation of documentation used in the review process. The framework discussions emphasized a three-tier planning approach—national, regional and local— and cross-sector alignment across areas such as hydrology, climate change, biodiversity, transport, legal issues and Adaptive Delta Management.

Stakeholder engagement

Three workshops supporting mainstream spatial planning

Workshop coordination

Cross-sector stakeholder workshops

Helped organize and attend three stakeholder workshops designed to explore how spatial planning could be mainstreamed into Bangladesh's national development planning process.

  • Private-sector organizations and professional bodies
  • Government ministries, departments and implementing agencies
  • Representatives from divisions of the Bangladesh Planning Commission
  • Workshop logistics, coordination, documentation and follow-up support
Planning objective

Linking spatial and development planning

The workshop process focused on improving coordination between spatial planning and economic development decisions, with particular attention to climate resilience, land-use management, infrastructure, settlement patterns and national development priorities under BDP 2100.
