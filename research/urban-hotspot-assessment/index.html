Urban Hotspot Assessment in Bangladesh Using Multi-Source Remote Sensing

An ongoing national-scale study mapping urban surface-heat patterns across Bangladesh using multi-source Earth-observation and settlement data.

Research in progress
Focus District-level comparison of surface heat, vegetation, built-up intensity, nighttime activity and population across Bangladesh.
Analytical units 64 districts
