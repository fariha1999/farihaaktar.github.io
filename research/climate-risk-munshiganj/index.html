Satellite-Based Climate Risk Assessment of Munshiganj Municipality

This assessment was conducted as part of the preparation of a Climate Action Plan for a small town in Bangladesh. It uses GIS and Earth-observation data to assess land-cover change, surface heat, rainfall variation, flood vulnerability, and integrated climate risk at the municipal scale.

Research focus

Assessing climate risk from Earth observation

Research objective

Identify spatial patterns of climate exposure across Munshiganj Municipality by integrating land use/land cover, land surface temperature, rainfall and flood vulnerability.

Analytical question

Which wards experience overlapping environmental and climate stresses, and where should climate adaptation interventions be prioritized?

Methodology

Satellite data + GIS-based overlay assessment

Land Use / Land Cover1995 Landsat 5 TM Surface Reflectance (30 m) and 2025 Sentinel-2 MSI Surface Reflectance (10 m).
Land Surface TemperatureThermal satellite data from Landsat 5 (1995) and Landsat 8 (2025).
RainfallCHIRPS daily rainfall data used to assess spatial rainfall variation.
Flood vulnerabilityWeighted overlay integrating DEM, river proximity, rainfall, slope, LULC and soil conditions.
Integrated climate riskLULC, LST, rainfall and flood vulnerability were overlaid to identify areas experiencing multiple climate stresses.
